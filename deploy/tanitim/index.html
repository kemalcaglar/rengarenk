About Rengarenk

Rengarenk is a reverse classifieds marketplace for vehicles in Türkiye. Instead of browsing thousands of listings, a buyer publishes what they are looking for (make, model, year, mileage, budget, city) as a request. Dealerships and individual sellers whose inventory matches are notified and message the buyer directly.

Dealerships subscribe to monthly plans (Starter, Professional, Enterprise) to receive matched requests and contact buyers; buyers get their first request free and can purchase extra requests or promotion. Rengarenk does not take a commission on vehicle sales.

The product (web, iOS and Android apps, and an operations console) is built on a TypeScript stack (NestJS, Next.js, Expo, PostgreSQL, OpenSearch) and is preparing for a closed beta in Türkiye.
